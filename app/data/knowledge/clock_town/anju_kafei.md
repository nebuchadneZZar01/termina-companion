# Anju & Kafei Quest

Anju is waiting for her fiancé Kafei at the Stock Pot Inn. Kafei has been
turned into a child by the Skull Kid and is hiding near the Laundry Pool. The
quest spans all three days and combines dialogue windows, mail delivery,
inventory items, a pendant and a late-night reunion.

## Main route

### First Day — begin the search

1. Between **10:00 AM and 12:00 PM**, enter the Mayor's Residence in East Clock
   Town and speak with Madame Aroma through the right-hand door. Offer to help
   find Kafei. She gives you **Kafei's Mask**.
2. Between **2:10 PM and 8:00 PM**, speak to Anju at the Stock Pot Inn while
   wearing Kafei's Mask. The interaction can be triggered after the Postman's
   delivery or later in the same window. Anju asks you to meet her in the inn's
   kitchen at **11:30 PM**.
3. At the night meeting, Anju gives you **Kafei's Letter**. Put it in a
   Clock Town mailbox before the Postman's morning collection. The letter must
   reach the Postman early enough for the Day 2 delivery.

The room key is an optional but important shortcut. Anju can give it during
the early Stock Pot Inn interaction, allowing access to the inn after normal
closing hours. Without it, the player can still complete the route by using the
Song of Double Time and waiting outside, but the key removes an unnecessary
wait.

### Second Day — meet Kafei

1. Between **6:00 AM and 9:00 AM**, place Kafei's Letter in any Clock Town
   mailbox.
2. Around **3:10 PM**, go to the Laundry Pool and wait for the Postman to ring
   the Curiosity Shop bell and deliver the letter. The guide commonly lists a
   **3:10 PM–3:50 PM** arrival window; exact presentation varies by version.
3. After Kafei returns to the back room, enter between **4:10 PM and 6:00 PM**
   and speak with him. He explains the child transformation and gives you the
   **Pendant of Memories**.

### Second or Third Day — convince Anju to wait

Give the Pendant of Memories to Anju at the Stock Pot Inn between **10:00 AM
and 7:00 PM** on Day 2 or Day 3. The pendant communicates Kafei's intentions
and prevents Anju from leaving Clock Town before the final night. Giving it on
Day 2 leaves time for an optional scene in Anju's room later that night.

### Final Night — reunion

After the pendant has been delivered, return to the employee room at the Stock
Pot Inn before dawn on the Final Day. Kafei eventually arrives and the couple
reunites. The **Couple's Mask** is awarded when the route has been completed.
Wait in the room rather than assuming that a failed meeting can be fixed after
the Moon falls.

## Optional branches

- On the Final Day, the Curiosity Shop owner can give Link the **Keaton Mask**
  and a **Special Delivery to Mama** during the Day 3 back-room visit. The
  delivery can later be given to Madame Aroma at the Milk Bar.
- Kafei can retrieve his stolen **Sun's Mask** at Sakon's Hideout in Ikana
  Canyon. This is a separate, tightly timed branch and is not required for the
  basic Stock Pot Inn reunion. It requires additional travel and puzzle
  equipment, so a planner should not silently insert it into a simple Clock
  Town goal.
- The Postman's Hat and other Bombers' Notebook rewards are optional. They
  should be modeled as optional objectives with their own evidence, not as
  prerequisites for the core reunion.

## Deadlines and failure modes

Missing the first-day Mask conversation, mailing the letter too late, or
arriving after Kafei has left loses the relevant chance until the three-day
cycle is reset. A planner should therefore check the current day, the
mailbox cutoff, the Laundry Pool delivery and the final room together. The
RAG document supplies narrative evidence; MCP objective windows remain the
authoritative scheduling source.
