# Curiosity Shop & Laundry Pool

The Curiosity Shop and the Laundry Pool are adjacent gameplay spaces in Clock
Town. The shop's back room opens into the Laundry Pool area, where Kafei uses a
small hideout. The connection is easy to miss when a guide lists two different
map names.

## Locations

- The **Curiosity Shop** is in West Clock Town near the Trading Post. The same
  proprietor appears in the shop and in the back-room/hideout context.
- The **Laundry Pool** is a small closed area reached from South Clock Town. It
  contains water, a bench, a bell and the back-room entrance used by Kafei.
- A **Stray Fairy** is available at the Laundry Pool during the day; at night
  the relevant fairy moves to East Clock Town.
- **Guru-Guru** can be found at the Laundry Pool on the First or Second Night,
  where his music is connected to the Bremen Mask side activity.

## Kafei's letter and hideout

On Day 2, the Postman rings the Curiosity Shop bell at the Laundry Pool and
delivers Kafei's Letter. After the masked child returns inside, Link can enter
the back room during the later talk window and receive the Pendant of Memories.
This is a different event from simply entering the shop and buying an item.

On the Final Day, after the basic Anju/Kafei route has been completed, the
Curiosity Shop owner can give Link the **Keaton Mask** and the **Special
Delivery to Mama** from the back room. The delivery is optional and belongs to
the Bombers' Notebook happiness/errand chain, not to the mandatory reunion.

## Night access

The shop and related spaces have version-sensitive opening behavior. A plan
should use the game's clock and the event trigger rather than assume that every
interior is accessible whenever the district is reachable. When a guide gives
an opening time, preserve the source's version note.
