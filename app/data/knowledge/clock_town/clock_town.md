# Clock Town

Clock Town is Termina's central settlement and the main staging area for the
first three-day cycle. It is a compact hub with four connected districts:
North, East, West and South Clock Town. The town contains shops, services,
inns, residents and several timed errands. Most destinations outside town are
unlocked by story progress, so a route can become unavailable when the clock or
the current day changes.

## Time and reset loop

The player has three days before the Moon crisis must be resolved. Activities
repeat only when the player returns to the dawn of the First Day with the Song
of Time. The Song of Double Time advances to night or to the next dawn, while
the Inverted Song of Time slows the clock. These songs are useful for temporal
planning, but they are not substitutes for checking an NPC's actual schedule.

A plan should state both the day and the in-game time. A deadline at midnight
is different from a deadline at 6:00 AM on the following day, and a shop's
night-only hours should not be confused with an event that happens once during
a particular day.

## Districts and services

- **North Clock Town** contains the Great Fairy fountain, the playground area,
  the Mayor's route toward the northern exit and several Bombers. It is also a
  useful hub for the first Bombers' Notebook objective.
- **East Clock Town** contains the Mayor's Residence, the Milk Bar/Latte, the
  Treasure Chest Game and the Astral Observatory route. Madame Aroma starts the
  Anju & Kafei search here.
- **West Clock Town** contains the Stock Pot Inn, the Post Office, the Bomb
  Shop and the Trading Post/Curiosity Shop frontage. It is the practical area
  for Anju, the Postman and late-night shop errands.
- **South Clock Town** contains the Clock Tower entrance, the Laundry Pool
  approach, the lower paths and several early-game activities. The Laundry
  Pool is where Kafei's letter delivery and hideout events take place.

The exact labels vary between the original N64 release and Majora's Mask 3D.
When a route is version-sensitive, preserve the version note in the evidence
instead of presenting one schedule as universal.

## Useful early services

The Stock Pot Inn provides lodging and the Anju/Kafei quest rooms. The Post
Office handles the Postman's route and mailboxes. The Curiosity Shop and its
Laundry Pool back room are connected gameplay spaces, even when a player thinks
of them as separate destinations. The Bombers' hideout connects East Clock Town
to the Astral Observatory. These relationships are more useful to a planner
than a flat list of streets.

## Optional discoveries

A daytime Stray Fairy can be found at the Laundry Pool, while the nighttime
location moves to East Clock Town. The Bombers' Notebook records optional
problems and their time windows. These are good retrieval examples, but they
should not be mistaken for mandatory main objectives.
