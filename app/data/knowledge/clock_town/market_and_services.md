# Clock Town Market & Services

Clock Town contains a dense set of optional services. They are useful test
cases for a temporal planner because each service has its own location, opening
condition, currency cost or reward.

## Market and shopping

The Market District and the surrounding streets contain the Trading Post,
Honey & Darling's shop, the lottery shop, the Treasure Chest Game and the Town
Shooting Gallery. The Curiosity Shop is nearby in West Clock Town. Merchandise,
rupee costs and hours can vary by game version, so a document hit should be
treated as a lead and the structured item/quest data should decide whether an
interaction is feasible.

The Bomb Shop sells bombs and related supplies. Its owner and the North Clock
Town midnight thief encounter are separate from the Bombers' Notebook. A
player who asks for “a bomb” may need a purchase objective, a quest objective
or simply a location recommendation.

## Food, performance and money

The Milk Bar, also called Latte, is a restaurant and performance venue in East
Clock Town. It is the destination for several optional NPC deliveries and
performances. The Stock Pot Inn is the lodging service rather than a general
market. The Post Office and Postman provide mail services, while the Clock
Tower and Business Scrub interaction provide a separate title-deed side route.

## Reusable planning pattern

For a service goal, record:

- service name and canonical location;
- day/time availability;
- rupees or item cost;
- prerequisite story state;
- reward and whether it is optional.

This pattern applies to shops, minigames, mail and performance venues without
making the RAG layer authoritative over structured game constraints.
