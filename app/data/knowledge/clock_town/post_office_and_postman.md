# Post Office & Postman

The Post Office is in West Clock Town. The Postman lives there and uses
Clock Town mailboxes as the trigger for letters. Mail is a temporal transport
mechanic: placing an item in a mailbox is not enough if the Postman's collection
window has already passed.

## Office and collection windows

The office is open from approximately **3:00 PM to midnight** on the First and
Second Days. The Postman begins collecting mail in the morning and follows a
route through town. A quest plan should name the mailbox cutoff rather than
only saying “mail the letter”.

The Anju & Kafei route uses this sequence:

1. Anju gives Link Kafei's Letter during the First Day night meeting.
2. Link places it in a Clock Town mailbox before the Postman's Day 2 run,
   normally in the early-morning window before 9:00 AM.
3. Around 3:10 PM on Day 2, the Postman rings the bell at the Laundry Pool and
   delivers the letter to Kafei.

The route is different from the First Day delivery of Kafei's existing letter
to Anju at the Stock Pot Inn. A retriever should preserve that distinction so
that two similarly named letters are not collapsed.

## Postman's Hat

The Postman's Hat is an optional Bombers' Notebook reward. The reliable broad
route is to help with the Postman's delivery, especially the Special Delivery
to Mama, then speak with him after his run. The exact reward trigger and the
version-specific sequence should be checked against structured game data before
a planner marks it as required.

## Planning notes

For a temporal plan, record:

- current day and hour;
- the Post Office opening status;
- the exact mailbox deadline;
- the Laundry Pool bell/delivery window; and
- the downstream objective that consumes the letter.

The Postman is not a generic “mail NPC” whose schedule can be replaced by a
single all-day window. He is a moving trigger with route-specific events.
