# Mayor's Residence & Madame Aroma

The Mayor's Residence is in East Clock Town. It is the starting point for the
Anju & Kafei search and a hub for several Clock Town NPC errands. Madame Aroma
is Kafei's mother and helps stage the town's performances.

## Starting Anju & Kafei

On the First Day, enter the residence between **10:00 AM and 12:00 PM** and use
the right-hand door to speak with Madame Aroma. She explains that Kafei is
missing and gives Link **Kafei's Mask** to use while searching. This is the
quest's first required trigger.

The residence is not the same location as the Milk Bar. A plan should not send
the player to the performance venue for the initial Mask conversation unless
the objective has been grounded to the correct event.

## Optional residence content

- A conversation involving Madame Aroma, Toto and Gorman can be observed in the
  residence during a narrow late-morning window on the First Day.
- Mayor Dotour can be thanked with the Couple's Mask after the Anju & Kafei
  route; this is an optional reward.
- The Milk Bar/Latte can provide later optional deliveries, including the
  Special Delivery to Mama, after the relevant quest item has been obtained.

These are optional evidence-backed events. They should not be inserted into a
minimal “get Kafei's Mask” goal unless the player explicitly asks for optional
NPC assistance.

## Planning notes

Track the residence as a location with a purpose, not just a point on a map:

- `get_kafei_mask` requires Madame Aroma and a daytime window;
- `optional_residence_scene` is a different trigger;
- `mayor_dotour_thanks` is a reward after the Couple's Mask.

The distinction prevents a retrieval hit for “Mayor” from being mistaken for
the Mask objective.
