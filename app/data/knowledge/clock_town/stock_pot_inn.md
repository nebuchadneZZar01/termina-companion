# Stock Pot Inn

The Stock Pot Inn is the main lodging and family-work location in West Clock
Town. Anju manages the front desk, with help from her mother and grandmother.
The inn is also the anchor for the Anju & Kafei quest: the first conversation,
the late-night kitchen meeting, the pendant delivery and the final reunion all
take place here or in its connected rooms.

## Opening hours and access

The inn is open from approximately **8:00 AM to 8:30 PM**. A guest receives a
**Room Key**, which allows the player to enter and leave after the public
closing time. Anju can give Link the key during the early Day 1 interaction at
the front desk. The key is particularly useful for reaching the kitchen around
11:30 PM without repeatedly manipulating time.

The employee-only room upstairs becomes relevant on the Final Night. It is not
the same as a normal guest room, so a location model should distinguish the
front desk, kitchen, guest floor and employee room when it needs precise
routing.

## Residents and services

- **Anju** works at the front desk and manages guest relations. Her concern for
  Kafei drives the quest's investigation and reunion.
- **Anju's mother** helps run the inn and appears in family scenes.
- **Anju's grandmother** has optional stories and diary-related content. These
  are separate side activities, not requirements for the Kafei route.
- The inn provides a safe indoor location for waiting, but the player still
  has to respect the clock and quest trigger windows.

## Quest-relevant spaces

The kitchen is where Anju asks Link to meet her late on the First Day. The
employee room is where the final couple's ceremony takes place. Anju's room can
be used for an optional Day 2 scene if the Pendant of Memories has already been
delivered. The room layout matters because several goals use the broad label
“Stock Pot Inn” while actually requiring a specific room and time.

## Planning notes

A useful plan distinguishes:

1. public-hours interaction with Anju;
2. access to the inn after closing;
3. the 11:30 PM kitchen meeting;
4. delivery of the Pendant of Memories; and
5. the pre-dawn final-room wait.

These are separate temporal events even when they share one map location. The
structured knowledge base should own the exact objective windows; this
